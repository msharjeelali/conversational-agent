# model_links.txt
Model checkpoints are stored on Google Drive due to GitHub file size limits.

Download and place in the following paths:

1. DialoGPT fine-tuned
   Drive link: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tgVn0gWmkLDjzS8OFby1rBqIu_rDpdgt?usp=drive_link
   Local path: model/checkpoints/finetuned/

2. Intent classifier
   Drive link: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Vel-VtcxTGZNq-FBEJdZLUABk9Ut7tP-?usp=drive_link
   Local path: model/checkpoints/intent_classifier/

After downloading, your model/ directory should look like:
model/
└── checkpoints/
    ├── finetuned/
    │   ├── config.json
    │   ├── model.safetensors
    │   ├── tokenizer.json
    │   └── tokenizer_config.json
    └── intent_classifier/
        ├── config.json
        ├── model.safetensors
        ├── tokenizer.json
        └── tokenizer_config.json
